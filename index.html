Junior High School (JHS)/h3>

Junior High School (JHS) in the Philippines covers Grades 7 to 10 and serves as a critical transition period between elementary education and Senior High School. It is designed to strengthen foundational knowledge, develop higher-order thinking skills, and prepare students for more specialized learning in Senior High School or technical-vocational pathways.
